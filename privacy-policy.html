Who we are

Element Sound Studio (also known as Element Culture), run by RM Productions, is a recording studio and music production studio at 48/2123b, Meenakshi Kripa, Puliyat Lane, Peerandoor, Elamakkara, Kochi – 682026, Kerala, India. This policy explains how we handle information in connection with elementkochi.com.

Information we collect

This website does not have a sign-up process, user accounts, or a contact form. We do not collect or store any personal information through the site itself. When you reach out to us by phone, WhatsApp, or a social media direct message, that conversation is handled directly by us (or, in WhatsApp's case, also by WhatsApp/Meta as the messaging provider) and is used only to respond to your enquiry — for example, to answer a question about a program or confirm studio time.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers of any kind. If that changes in the future — for instance, if we add visit analytics to understand how the site is used — we will update this policy to describe what's collected and why.

Third-party links

Our site links out to services we don't control, including Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and Google Maps. Each of these has its own privacy policy governing how they handle your information, and we'd encourage you to review those if you have concerns before using them.

Data security

Because we don't collect or store personal data through the website, there's no visitor database to secure. Any details you share with us directly (by phone or WhatsApp, for example) are kept only as long as needed to handle your enquiry or booking, and are not sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Children's privacy

Our training programs are offered to students of various ages, including minors, typically enrolled with a parent or guardian's involvement. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website, as the website itself does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our services or this website change. The "last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.
