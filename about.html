The studio

Element Sound Studio by RM Productions is a recording studio and creative music hub based in Elamakkara, Kochi, offering professional recording alongside vocal, production and sound engineering training for artists of every level.

We’re proud to be a space with no auditions and no criteria — just a shared love of music and the discipline to grow.

Our mission is to be the sublime centre to inspire creativity and self-expression, with a vast selection of high-quality artistic excellence, responsible training and practices, while fostering a culture of innovation and passion for music and art.

Our values

Quality & Excellence — the finest, most diverse range of services, always improving.

Integrity & Ethics — honesty, transparency and fairness in every interaction.

Innovation & Adaptability — exploring new trends and technologies in music.

Sustainability — minimising our footprint, promoting responsible practice.

Collaboration & Partnerships — building a resilient creative ecosystem.

Compliance & Regulation — never compromising on industry standards.

To redefine a world where the enjoyment of music and art inspires creative learning — blending traditional artistry with modern innovation, with a lifelong passion for a vibrant artistic community that connects people, culture and ideas.

We strive to be the go-to hub for nurturing talent, the first choice for creativity, discipline and originality — a respected advocate for shaping confident, expressive creators of tomorrow.

Rooted in Kerala, tuned to the world

Kathakali's rhythm, the calm of the backwaters, the carved stillness of our temples — the same artistic discipline runs through everything we record. Element Sound Studio blends that inheritance with modern production, one session at a time.
