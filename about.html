We believe in the power of music and self-expression. Element Sound Studio by RM Productions brings responsible training and artistic excellence together under one roof, fostering a culture of innovation and passion for music and art.

The studio

Element Sound Studio by RM Productions is a recording studio and creative music hub based in Elamakkara, Kochi, offering professional recording alongside vocal, production and sound engineering training for artists of every level.

We’re proud to be a space with no auditions and no criteria — just a shared love of music and the discipline to grow.

Our mission is to be the sublime centre to inspire creativity and self-expression, with a vast selection of high-quality artistic excellence, responsible training and practices, while fostering a culture of innovation and passion for music and art.

Our values

Quality & Excellence — the finest, most diverse range of services, always improving.

Integrity & Ethics — honesty, transparency and fairness in every interaction.

Innovation & Adaptability — exploring new trends and technologies in music.

Sustainability — minimising our footprint, promoting responsible practice.

To redefine a world where the enjoyment of music and art inspires creative learning — blending traditional artistry with modern innovation, with a lifelong passion for a vibrant artistic community that connects people, culture and ideas.

We strive to be the go-to hub for nurturing talent, the first choice for creativity, discipline and originality — a respected advocate for shaping confident, expressive creators of tomorrow.

One studio, three names

The Element family

Beyond the studio walls, Element runs as a small creative family — a culture, a community and a label, all born from the same room.

Element Culture

Element Culture is the beating heart of the studio’s community — the open mics, listening sessions, workshops and masterclasses that bring Kochi’s musicians and artists together outside the booth.

We exist to give every artist, from first-timers to seasoned performers, a stage, an audience and a reason to keep creating — because music is meant to be shared, not just recorded.

Kochi’s Stage — a home for the city’s music and art community to gather and grow.

The Element Circle

The Element Circle is our creative collective — a rotating group of musicians, producers and artists who meet, collaborate and build together, inside and outside the studio.

We believe the best work happens in community, not isolation. The Circle exists to connect people who make things, so ideas travel further than any one artist could take them alone.

Create — hands-on sessions, jams and collaborative projects, from first idea to finished track.

Connect — a network of musicians, producers and creatives who show up for each other.

Cultivate — mentorship and shared knowledge that helps every member grow.

Element Records

Element Records is our in-house label — where the music made in this room gets released into the world, produced, mixed and mastered by the same team that trains here.

We exist to give original music, produced right here at the studio, a real home — released with the same care and standards we bring to every session.

Original Music — releases produced, mixed and mastered in-house, start to finish.

Artist-First — the same team and standards behind every session, on every release.

One Studio — training, recording and releasing, all under a single roof.
