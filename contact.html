Frequently asked questions

Where is Element Sound Studio located?

Element Sound Studio (Element Culture) is at 48/2123b, Meenakshi Kripa, Puliyat Lane, Peerandoor, Elamakkara, Kochi – 682026.

What services does Element Sound Studio offer?

Professional audio recording, mixing and mastering, vocal recording, sound engineering training and music production, plus hourly studio time for artists and bands.

How much does studio time cost?

Studio time is ₹1,250 per hour, covering recording, mixing and production space. See the Programs page for training fees.

What training programs are available?

Vocal training, vocal production, music production, sound engineering, Hindustani classical music, general music classes and crochet — see Programs for fees and schedules.

Does Element Sound Studio host live music events?

Yes — our events arm, Element Culture, hosts Open Mic nights, our Listen concert series and artist masterclasses for Kochi’s music and art community.

How can I book studio time or enrol in a program?

Call or WhatsApp us at +91 98953 14555, or visit the studio in Elamakkara, Kochi.
